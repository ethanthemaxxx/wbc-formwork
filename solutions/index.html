01Flows

Intake, matching, triage, close, claims and reporting.

Ilse Verhoeven, Head of Delivery

We draw the map first. If we cannot draw it, we cannot automate it.

01

Logistics

Document intake

Orders, manifests and invoices arrive as PDFs and emails. The flow reads them, checks them and files them.

In >
Email, PDF, scan
Flow >
Read, validate, match
Out >
Clean record, flagged gaps
Typical result
-70% hand keying. Illustrative.
02

Logistics

Dispatch and exceptions

Scans and carrier updates trigger rules. Exceptions go to the right lead with the facts attached.

In >
Scanner events, carrier feeds
Flow >
Classify, route, alert
Out >
Owner alerted, task opened
Typical result
-47% time to clear an exception. Illustrative.
03

Finance

Invoice matching

Invoices meet orders and receipts. Matches post. Mismatches wait for a person with the evidence.

In >
Invoice, order, receipt
Flow >
Three-way match, tolerance check
Out >
Posted entry, review queue
Typical result
-62% matching time. Illustrative.
04

Finance

Close and reconciliation

The flow reconciles accounts, drafts journals and writes the variance notes. A controller signs off.

In >
Ledger, bank, sub-ledgers
Flow >
Reconcile, draft, explain
Out >
Draft journals, variance notes
Typical result
-3 days on the close. Illustrative.
05

Support

Ticket triage and replies

Every ticket arrives tagged, ranked and half-answered. Agents spend their time on the hard ones.

In >
Inbox, chat, form
Flow >
Tag, rank, draft
Out >
Prioritised queue, draft reply
Typical result
-58% time to first reply. Illustrative.
06

Support / Finance

Claims and reporting

Claims are checked against evidence. Weekly reports write themselves from the logs.

In >
Claim form, photos, order data
Flow >
Verify, route, summarise
Out >
Decision draft, weekly report
Typical result
-4 hrs per week on reporting. Illustrative.

02Process

Four steps from map to measure.

Nothing is built before it is mapped.

.01

Map the process

We shadow the team for one to two weeks. We draw every step, handoff and exception.

.02

Frame the flow

We choose what to automate and what stays human. You sign off on the plan before any build.

.03

Pour to production

We build, test on real cases and go live in stages. A person reviews the first runs.

.04

Cure and hand over

We measure against the baseline, document the flow and train your owner.
